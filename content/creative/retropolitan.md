An interior stylist and myself built eight rooms in a studio and needed them to read as one place, not eight jobs. One story.

Every wall, prop, and piece of furniture was built or dressed for the shoot. The photography's job was to get out of the way of that work and still make you want to walk in.

Sets and interiors: Amy Heimermann
