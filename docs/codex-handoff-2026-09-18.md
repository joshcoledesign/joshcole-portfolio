What belongs where, so nothing drifts:

- A **study** is `kind: study` (the default) and lives only in `content/case-studies/`. Its body is the page. `thumbnail` (grid asset) and `heroImage` are separate; the map uses `thumbnail` first.
- A **series** or **single** is `kind: series | single` and lives only in `content/creative/`. Its `images` list is the page; the body is the prose below the frames. `thumbnail` is the map/mosaic image and never reorders `images`.
- **`published: false`** hides a piece everywhere: route 404, off the map, out of `ls`, out of the photography block, out of every count. Today that is `identity-pipeline.md` (retired 2026-09-19, folded into Novensia), `black-13.md`, `love-and-flak.md`. `nemo.md` was published 2026-09-20 after client approval and takes Identity Pipeline's old slot (weight 2, order 11).
- **Bodies are never edited by build agents.** Frontmatter is editable only where this doc or a brief asks. Copy fixes are `TODO(josh):` comments in frontmatter.
- **Counts are computed** from the loader, never typed: the status bar, the role-line paragraph, the photography caption.
- Anything under `.claude/worktrees/`, `public/gallery/`, or the Vercel Blob gallery is not content and is not read by the loader once step 1 lands.
