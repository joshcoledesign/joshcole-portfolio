## Photography: rules that were decided

- Photography-tagged series collapse into one **Photography** block on the unfiltered map (a 3×3 mosaic that must show images; item 17). `--photography` or `--creative-direction` releases them as tiles. Saints and Fractured are their own home tiles.
- Series = the shoot or the concept, named as a human would. Tags = the kind, from a short list Josh keeps consistent. No series called "Portraits."
- Mosaic cell labels are each series' `title`; the block caption's category words are computed from tags, never typed.
- Singles (one-frame pieces) live inside the block only; `single` in the frame-count field.
- Review convention: images sent with no prompt = "should this go on the site?"; a single image is judged as a single; a yes comes with a pre-filled line.
- Line format: `Name | one-line descriptor (<15 words) | frame count or single | tags | thumbnail filename | credits / notes`
