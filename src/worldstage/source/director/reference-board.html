What we are applying

  • Metal nozzle, outlet and plume share one pivot and direction.
  • Ignition unloads the suspension before liftoff; hover requires support against gravity.
  • Acceleration follows force. A pitched ship can retain forward velocity while its engines return toward vertical.
  • Dust starts while thrust is near the ground and continues drifting after the craft leaves.

Physics in this prototype

Force-drivenStill simplified
New landings and takeoff integrate mass, gravity, actual thrust vector, actuator lag, gimbal limits, gear springs/dampers, friction and bounded thrust-imbalance torque at 120 Hz.Planar motion, fixed mass and stylized wind. Seven visible nozzles use a lumped thrust model, not seven independently solved engine assemblies.
Earlier landing records retain their saved forces, contact and suspension response.Those captures include independent horizontal correction; a visual rig does not retroactively make their forces vector-coupled.
Thrust and wind drive illustrated exhaust, smoke, ground wash and sound.Plume shape, combustion, heat shimmer and dust are artistic layers, not fluid dynamics or chemical simulation.
